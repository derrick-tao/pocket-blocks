Playfield gestures: tap the left half to rotate counterclockwise or the right half to rotate clockwise. Each tap turns once. Swipe horizontally to move.

Drag down slowly to soft drop and steer sideways; a brisk vertical flick hard drops. Swipe up to hold or swap once per piece. Keep gestures within the board. Holding still does not repeat movement. Two fingers cancel a gesture.

Prefer buttons? Choose Visible buttons under Game options → Touch controls. Hold those buttons to repeat movement. Keyboard controls always remain available.

Clear 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 lines for 40 / 100 / 300 / 1,200 points × level. Every 10 lines raises your level and speeds up gravity, from 800 ms per row toward an 80 ms minimum. Pieces lock on a blocked downward step; rotations need room.

In Game options, choose your touch controls, a landing outline, and line-clear celebrations. These choices do not change scoring or speed.

Classic-inspired, with a 10×20 board and seven shapes. It uses simple rotation and independent random pieces, rather than modern wall kicks, a seven-piece bag or lock delay. It does not emulate one historical edition exactly.

Best score and options stay in this browser when local storage is available. Sound is optional.

Keep this page open online until “Ready to play offline” appears. The first visit needs internet; installation is optional.

iPhone or iPad: open this page in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. Enable Open as Web App if offered, then tap Add. Open the installed Home Screen app once online and wait for “Ready to play offline” there too; Safari and Home Screen storage may be separate. iOS does not provide this game a programmatic install prompt.

Other browsers may offer Install app or Add to Home Screen in their menu. Your browser controls availability.

Once downloaded, reopen this page or Home Screen app without Wi-Fi to play. Browser data cleanup, private browsing, or storage eviction may remove the download. Saved scores and options belong to this browser/device. Reopening starts a fresh ready screen.

The game requests a fixed mobile zoom level to reduce accidental gestures while playing. Browser and OS accessibility settings may override this. Scrolling and normal controls remain available.
